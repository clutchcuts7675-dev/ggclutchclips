Privacy Policy

GG Clutch Clips Uploader · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who this applies to

GG Clutch Clips Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the GG Clutch Clips channel to publish the channel's own videos. It is not available to the public, and it does not collect data from anyone else.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's GG Clutch Clips channel, and to read the channel's own upload list so the same video is never posted twice.
  • Gmail (Google): permission to send email notifications from the owner's account to the owner (for example, "this clip was posted").
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the owner's @gg.clutch.clips account and read its own posts to avoid duplicates.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository, and are used only to perform the actions above.
  • The app does not read email content, contacts, or any personal data.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Instagram/Facebook settings under connected apps.
